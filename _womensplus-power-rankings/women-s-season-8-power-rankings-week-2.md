      Black spent most of Sunday stuck on the runway, determined to make “pitch”
      happen, running the same pitch play over and over despite Sapphire

      sides of the entire game without a single sub. And if Black could score as
      much as they complained to the refs, then maybe that \#1 spot could have
      been theirs. But for now, Midnight wins the battle of the Snatches and
      takes the W. They may have gotten off the ground, but this flight was
      definitely running behind schedule.
